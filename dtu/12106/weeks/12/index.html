01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

Essential takeaways, synthesised from the module and case instructions.

01

Transitions involve connected systems

Changing one technology can alter demand, infrastructure and behaviour. Use a systemic perspective to identify interactions that a narrow product comparison may miss.

02

Feedbacks and delays affect outcomes

System-dynamics exercises explore how interventions work over time. A promising immediate change can have different longer-term consequences when reinforcing or balancing effects are included.

03

Interpret dimensions together

The assigned assessment-interpretation reading supports presenting combined results. Keep conflicting indicators and uncertainty visible instead of hiding them in one unsupported sustainability score.

FOR YOUR MICROFABRICATION PROJECT

Complete individual Task 2 peer feedback by Wednesday 25 November. Use received feedback to identify revisions for Task 3.

02 / PRACTISE

Map a small transition pathway

30–60 min · Suggested

For one proposed improvement in your project, draw a causal chain with one feedback loop or delay. Add a three-column table of environmental, economic and social effects, including uncertainties.

FINISH WITH

One diagram and a balanced interpretation to help structure Task 3.

QUICK SELF-CHECK

Explain the direction of each connection and distinguish established evidence from a hypothesis.

Suggested pace: 10 minutes on the essentials, 20–40 minutes on the task, 5–10 minutes checking your result. Mandatory assignment work is additional.

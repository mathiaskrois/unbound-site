01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

Essential takeaways, synthesised from the module and case instructions.

01

Integrate rather than repeat

Task 3 combines prior scope and calculations into a multidimensional conclusion. Use Tasks 1 and 2 as foundations, then explain what they jointly establish about sustainability.

02

Evaluate safe operating space

Discuss the case’s allocated share and global significance where supportable. Make the allocation and scale assumptions explicit; uncertain data should limit the strength of the conclusion.

03

Explain systemic trade-offs

Consider circular-economy opportunities and interactions among the three pillars. Show where a proposed improvement shifts burdens or depends on wider system conditions.

04

A good conclusion states its limits

The case criteria emphasise critical evaluation, a systemic perspective, uncertainty and integration. Explain what remains unknown and whether it could change the recommendation.

FOR YOUR MICROFABRICATION PROJECT

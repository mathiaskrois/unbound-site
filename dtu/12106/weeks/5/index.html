01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

Essential takeaways, synthesised from the module and case instructions.

01

Health effects need their own assessment

Climate emissions alone do not describe human health burdens. The module connects inventory-based health impacts, disease burdens and effects on users. Keep the mechanism and indicator clear.

02

Include harms and benefits consistently

The mobility exercise includes health effects of physical activity as well as burdens. An assessment can omit a major effect if it looks only at emissions from a process.

03

One inventory supports several indicators

The mobility workbook contains hidden health and ecosystem columns. Expanding them reuses the inventory; it does not remove uncertainty in activity data or characterisation factors.
